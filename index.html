FAQs

Questions customers ask us

Can't find your answer? Our team is a call or WhatsApp message away.

Contact us WhatsApp
How fast can you pick up within Asaba?

For local deliveries we usually dispatch a rider shortly after you book during business hours. Same-day delivery is standard within Asaba and nearby towns like Okpanam and Ibusa.

Do you deliver outside Delta State?

Yes. We deliver to all 36 states and the FCT, including Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt, Enugu, Onitsha and Benin, with express and economy options.

Can you collect payment from my customers?

Yes. Our e-commerce delivery includes pay-on-delivery collection. We collect from your customer and remit the money back to you.

How do I track my parcel?

Every booking gets a tracking number on the waybill. Enter it on our Track page, or send it to us on WhatsApp for a live update.

What can't you deliver?

We don't carry illegal items, hazardous materials, weapons, or live animals. If you're unsure about an item, call us before booking.

How is pricing worked out?

Pricing depends on distance, size and weight, and speed of delivery. Use our quote form for an estimate, and we'll confirm the exact price before pickup.
