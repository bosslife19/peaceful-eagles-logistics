Rising above the stress of delivery

Peaceful Eagles Logistics is an Asaba logistics company helping individuals, sellers and businesses move what matters, safely and on time.

Our story

A logistics company with an eagle's view

The eagle flies above the storm. That idea sits at the heart of PELS: we take on the pressure of getting things where they need to go, so our customers can move on with their day.

From our office at Faith Akpede Plaza in GRA Phase 2, Asaba, we run a growing network of riders, vans and partners that connects Delta State to the rest of Nigeria and to the world.

Whether it's a single envelope across town or an entire shop's stock heading to Lagos, every job gets the same attention: careful packing, clear communication, and a delivery you can track.
